How it works

From a blank map to a proper briefing

What VirtualDispatch is, what it does today, where it's going, and how to fly your first leg.

What it is

A dispatch desk for your simulator

VirtualDispatch turns a flight in Microsoft Flight Simulator into an airline operation. You pick a real scheduled route flown by Ryanair, easyJet or Wizz Air, and it prepares what a dispatcher would hand you: live weather at both ends, an aircraft and load to plan for, delays and equipment defects to work around, and a loadsheet to sign before departure.

The goal is simple: every leg should start with decisions to make, not a blank map — so flying the sim feels like doing the job, not just moving the aircraft.

Key features

Real airline networks, on a map

Over 13,000 real scheduled routes from Ryanair, easyJet and Wizz Air. Search by airport or pick one straight on the route map, then narrow by airline, sector legs, or domestic and international flights.

{{ step_shot(shots, 'map') }}

Live aviation weather

Current METARs and TAFs for your departure and destination, plus optional map layers: every airport's flight category (VFR to LIFR) and active SIGMETs for thunderstorms, turbulence, icing and more.

{{ step_shot(shots, 'weather') }}

A real dispatch briefing

  • ScheduleOff-block, take-off, landing and in-block times, scheduled and expected.
  • Aircraft and loadAn aircraft from the fleet you fly, expected passengers, cargo and cost index.
  • Delays and MELsRealistic delay codes and equipment deferrals with real dispatch consequences — weather delays only when the weather supports them.
{{ step_shot(shots, 'briefing') }}

Loadsheet and SimBrief handoff

Sign the loadsheet, including last-minute changes and dangerous goods, send the flight to SimBrief in one click, and fetch the OFP back — with a warning if its aircraft doesn't match the one you were assigned.

{{ step_shot(shots, 'flight') }}

PIREP log and flying stats

File a PIREP at the end of each leg. Your log, flight hours, monthly activity and a map of every destination you've reached build up as you fly.

{{ step_shot(shots, 'profile') }}

Make it yours

Set your preferred base and the aircraft you fly, and switch each kind of complication — delays, last-minute changes, MELs — on or off to match how much of a challenge you want.

{{ step_shot(shots, 'settings') }}
