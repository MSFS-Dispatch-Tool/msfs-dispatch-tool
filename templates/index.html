DISPATCH TERMS

FLIGHT TYPE & ROUTE

1L 1L
One-Leg — a single, direct itinerary with no connection.
DOM DOM
Domestic — departure and arrival airports are in the same country.
INT INT
International — departure and arrival airports are in different countries.
ML ML
Multi-Leg — an itinerary of two connecting sectors flown back to back.

SCHEDULED TIMES

SIBT
Scheduled In-Block Time — scheduled gate arrival.
SLDT
Scheduled Landing Time — SIBT minus taxi-in.
SOBT
Scheduled Off-Block Time — scheduled gate departure.
STOT
Scheduled Take-Off Time — SOBT plus taxi-out.

EXPECTED / PLANNED FIGURES

EBT
Expected Block Time, shown in the itinerary list — the span from the first leg's departure to the last leg's arrival, including any ground time between legs.
EET
Estimated Elapsed Time — airborne time only (SLDT minus STOT).
EFOB
Estimated Fuel On Board at block-off (i.e. block/ramp fuel), from the SimBrief OFP.
EOBT / ETOT / ELDT / EIBT
Expected Off-Block/Take-Off/Landing/In-Block Time — the SOBT/STOT/SLDT/SIBT figures shifted by the leg's delay, if any. Equal to the scheduled figure when there's no delay. This is what's sent to SimBrief, not the raw scheduled time.
EPAX
Estimated passenger count used in the SimBrief OFP. See PAX below for how it's set.
ETOW
Estimated Take-Off Weight, from the SimBrief OFP.
EZFW
Estimated Zero Fuel Weight, from the SimBrief OFP.

LOAD & PERFORMANCE — HOW THEY'RE SET

CARGO
Expected checked/hold baggage weight (this tool doesn't model belly freight). Scaled off the leg's passenger count and sector length — longer sectors see a higher share of passengers checking a bag — with some built-in randomness, plus an occasional high-cargo "surge" leg.
COST INDEX
Randomly rolled per leg between 2 and 16 (centered around 6). In real dispatch this balances fuel cost against time cost for the FMC's speed/altitude optimization; it's sent to SimBrief to generate the OFP.
PAX
The aircraft's seat capacity (189 on Ryanair's 737-800, 156–235 across easyJet's A320 family and 180–239 across Wizz Air's, depending on type) times a randomized load factor (roughly 82–99%, with a 30% chance of a completely full house) gives the expected passenger count used to build the OFP (EPAX) and, ultimately, the loadsheet.

ACTUALS & PIREP

ABIT
Actual In-Block Time (block in), entered when filing the PIREP.
AFAD
Actual Fuel At Destination, entered when filing the PIREP — not to be confused with EFOB, which is the block fuel planned before departure.
ALDT
Actual Landing Time, entered when filing the PIREP.
AOBT
Actual Off-Block Time (block off), entered when filing the PIREP.
ATOT
Actual Take-Off Time, entered when filing the PIREP.
PIREP
Pilot Report — filed after block-in to close out the leg.
ZFW / FOB / PAX / CREW
Actual figures you enter at loadsheet sign-off — Zero Fuel Weight, Fuel On Board, passenger count, crew count.

OPERATIONAL EVENTS

LMC
Last-Minute Change — a late change to pax/cargo figures after the loadsheet was prepared.
MEL
Minimum Equipment List — a defect the aircraft is dispatched with under an MEL relief, with an associated operational consequence.

BEFORE YOU DISPATCH

This app doesn't tell SimBrief which aircraft to use. On the dispatch form's Aircraft Info section, set the Aircraft Type yourself — and the specific Variant or Airframe too, if you have one you'd rather fly — before generating the OFP.

Reviewing the generated flight plan is your own responsibility. Only press Generate on SimBrief once you're satisfied the route, fuel, and weights all look right.
