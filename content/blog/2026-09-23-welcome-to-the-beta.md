# Welcome to the VirtualDispatch beta

VirtualDispatch is now open for beta testing. If you're reading this, you probably found your way here from a signup link or a message from me directly &mdash; thanks for trying it out.

## What this is

VirtualDispatch generates real dispatch data for Ryanair 737-800 flights in Microsoft Flight Simulator: routes pulled from the real network, live weather, loadsheets, randomized MELs and delays, and a PIREP log to keep track of what you've flown. The goal is to make every leg start with a proper briefing instead of a blank map.

## What's still in progress

This is a beta, and a few things are still being built out:

- More granular control over which complications (MELs, delays, last-minute changes) show up
- Additional airports and route data as the underlying dataset grows
- Polishing the mobile experience

## Feedback

If you hit a bug, or something feels off, let me know &mdash; beta feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
